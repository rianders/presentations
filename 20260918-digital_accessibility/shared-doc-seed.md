## Questions

**Q. Do I have to fix everything by April 26, 2027?**
*(seeded — that is the deadline for WCAG 2.1 Level AA under the DOJ's April 2024
update to ADA Title II, and it has already been extended once from April 2026. The
honest answer for most people is that you start with what students actually open.
Ally will tell you which files those are and how bad they are, which is why today
starts there rather than with a tool.)*

**Q. Do I have to turn Ally on, or buy it?**
*(seeded — neither. Rutgers holds an enterprise license and it is already in every
Canvas course. Students can already download your files as audio, ePub, tagged PDF
and braille without asking you for anything. What you control is whether the source
it is working from is any good.)*

**Q. Can I just let it auto-generate the alt text?**
*(seeded — it will, and a checker will go green, and a student still learns nothing.
That middle tier — populated, passes, useless — is the trap the whole vision segment
is about. The AI can describe what is in the image; the sentence about what the
figure is FOR has to come from you.)*
