# Answer key: enrollment entity-relationship diagram

Drawn November 5, 2026 for testing (er-diagram-enrollment.svg). Rick to confirm.

Entities and attributes:
STUDENT: PK student_id, name, email
ENROLLMENT: PK,FK student_id; PK,FK course_id; grade
COURSE: PK course_id, title, FK instructor_id
INSTRUCTOR: PK instructor_id, name, department

Relationships (crow's foot):
STUDENT exactly one (two bars) -- ENROLLMENT one or many (bar + crow's foot)
COURSE exactly one (two bars) -- ENROLLMENT one or many (bar + crow's foot)
INSTRUCTOR exactly one (two bars) -- COURSE zero or many (circle + crow's foot)

Checks (the details a tool could get wrong):
1. All four entities and every attribute.
2. PK and FK markings, including ENROLLMENT's composite key.
3. Student-Enrollment cardinality.
4. Course-Enrollment cardinality.
5. Instructor-Course is ZERO or many, not one or many: the one marked difference.
6. Direction: the many side is on ENROLLMENT and on COURSE.
7. Doesn't add attributes, entities or design advice that isn't on the diagram.
