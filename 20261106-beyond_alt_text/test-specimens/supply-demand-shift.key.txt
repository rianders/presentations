# Answer key: supply and demand with a shift

Drawn November 5, 2026 for testing (supply-demand-shift.svg). Illustrative numbers.

Axes: Price ($) vertical, 0 to 12; Quantity horizontal, 0 to 100.
S: straight, rising, (0,0) to (100,10).
D1: straight, falling, (0,8) to (80,0).
D2: dashed, falling, parallel to D1, to the right of it, (20,10) to (100,2).
Arrow: at price 7, between D1 and D2, pointing right (from D1 toward D2).
E1: S crosses D1 at quantity 40, price 4. E2: S crosses D2 at quantity 60, price 6.
Dotted guides run from E1 and E2 to both axes.

Checks (the details a tool could get wrong):
1. Axes, labels, ranges.
2. S rises; D1 and D2 fall.
3. D2 is dashed, and to the right of D1.
4. E1 at (40, 4).
5. E2 at (60, 6).
6. The arrow's direction, D1 toward D2.
7. Doesn't add a cause, or say what "will happen" ("consumers want more", "so prices rise because…").
