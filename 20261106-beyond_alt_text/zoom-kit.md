## Zoom description

```
Beyond Alt Text: Notation, Diagrams & Symbols

Equations, chemical formulas, music, maps and diagrams: the notation experts read differently than they'd ever say aloud. See and hear what screen readers and AI tools actually do with it, what goes wrong, and how to check it.

For the first hour, we look at how to turn images of notation into something a screen reader can use in Canvas, which AI models and prompts worked best in our tests, and where they fail. In the last 30 minutes, we work through your examples together as a group.

Bring one page or figure from your course that you want tested.

Slides: https://rianders.github.io/presentations/shell.html?p=20261106-beyond_alt_text/beyond-alt-text
```

## Request for examples (send before the session)

```
Subject: Bring an example to Beyond Alt Text, November 6

For the last 30 minutes of Beyond Alt Text, we'll test real course materials together as a group. What would you like us to try?

Find one or two examples you'd like worked on: a page of handwritten notes, an equation sheet, a diagram or chart, or notation from your field that gives you trouble. Any discipline is welcome.

Please:
- Have it ready as an image or PDF, one page if you can.
- Remove student names, grades and other personal information. Use only material you're free to share with the group.
- Tell us what you want to know: Can AI read this? Will a screen reader read it correctly? How should this be described?

Post your example in the Zoom chat during the session, or email it ahead to rick.anderson@uoes.rutgers.edu. We'll choose from what comes in, and we'll work as many as time allows.
```
